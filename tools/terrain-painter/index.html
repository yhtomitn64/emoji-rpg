Wilderness Terrain Painter

Loads all 25 wilderness screens onto one continuous canvas, laid out exactly like the real 5x5 world, so terrain painted across a screen boundary is connected by construction. The "Map" dropdown switches to the dragon dungeon or a mini-dungeon variant instead — each is a standalone map with its own palette, painted the same way. Pick a brush, click or click-drag to paint. Export copies a map's LEGEND/ROWS to the clipboard, ready to paste over its existing declaration in its file (wilderness screens live under js/maps/wilderness/; the dungeon and mini-dungeons live under js/maps/). "Place Dungeon Entrance" marks the one fixed spot every save's dungeon spawns at (only meaningful on the wilderness map) — "Copy position" copies the exact value to paste into DEFAULT_DUNGEON_ENTRANCE_POSITION in js/state.js. "Check Map" flood-fills from the town entrance and tints anything not freely walkable: yellow needs a tool (axe/pick) to reach, red isn't reachable even with every tool — and tells you plainly whether the dungeon entrance itself is reachable without a tool (it has to be, since the axe/pick only drop inside the dungeon). Dungeon-interior maps don't have their own reachability check yet. "Place Tool Dungeon Entrance" works the same way for whichever tool dungeon is selected in the dropdown next to it — copies paste into that entry's screenId/x/y in js/data/toolDungeons.js.
