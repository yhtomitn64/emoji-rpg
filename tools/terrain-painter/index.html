Wilderness Terrain Painter

Loads all 25 wilderness screens onto one continuous canvas, laid out exactly like the real 5x5 world, so terrain painted across a screen boundary is connected by construction. Pick a brush, click or click-drag to paint. Export copies one screen's LEGEND/ROWS to the clipboard, ready to paste over that screen's existing declaration in its file under js/maps/wilderness/. "Place Dungeon Entrance" marks the one fixed spot every save's dungeon spawns at — "Copy position" copies the exact value to paste into DEFAULT_DUNGEON_ENTRANCE_POSITION in js/state.js.
